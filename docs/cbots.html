Core mechanic — one line triggers another

The entire system runs on a layered trigger model. A line doesn't just sit on the chart — it activates the next one. Break a structure level and the entry line arms. Touch the entry line and the trade fires. Miss the target and the system resets — quietly, automatically.

GapTrader — under the hood

A fully automated trading bot for range-bound markets. Perfect for sailors, remote workers, and anyone who needs "set-and-forget" trading. Works with Matrix integration for complete automation.

8-color line system

  • BLUE/RED lines — entry boxes (buy zones / sell zones)
  • WHITE/PURPLE lines — activation signals (optional confirmation)
  • GREEN line — liquidity gate (advanced mode safety)
  • ORANGE/PINK lines — profit-taking zones
  • YELLOW line — emergency kill switch (stops all trading)

Entry modes

  • ImmediateBreakout — trade box breakouts instantly
  • PullbackOnly — wait for pullback to the lines
  • Either — both breakout and pullback entries
  • MiddleBox — only trade the middle 40% of the box (safer entries, fewer fakeouts)

Risk management

  • Micro lot support — trade from 0.01 lots
  • Box edge stop loss — automatic SL at box boundaries
  • Risk/reward TP — configurable 1:1 to 5:1 targets
  • Partial close — take 50% profit at TP1, let the rest run
  • Break-even — auto-move SL after +X pips
  • Max trades per box & daily trade limit

Safety & filters

  • Box invalidation — auto-stop if structure breaks
  • Stagnation filter — pauses stale boxes
  • Spread filter — blocks high-spread periods
  • Time filter & Monday gap filter
  • Candle color & momentum (EMA slope) filters
  • Optional news filter integration

3 sequence modes

  • Basic (2–4 lines) — buy box + sell box, trade on breakout, no confirmation required
  • Simple (3–6 lines) — activation line must be hit before the box trades, fewer false signals
  • Advanced (4–8 lines) — liquidity gate first, activation second, trade last. Highest quality setups only

Matrix integration

When paired with MatrixBasketHybrid: Matrix analyzes currency strength across W1/D1/H4/H1, broadcasts a BUY or SELL signal, and GapTrader auto-places the entry lines (BUY: blue lines below price · SELL: red lines above price) — then trades them automatically.

Toggle it off and you place lines manually. On = complete automation.
